FAQ

Answers for prospective parents

Common questions about school life, our curriculum and Islamiyyah. For admissions, see our Admissions page.

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Nigerian curriculum, taught through enquiry-based learning, problem-solving, independent thinking, practical investigations and continuous feedback.

Which classes do you run?

Playgroup to Nursery 2 for ages 2 to 6, and Primary (Basic 1 to 5) for ages 6 to 11. Secondary, JSS 1 to SS 3, opens in the 2027/2028 session.

How is Islamic learning part of the school day?

From Early Years, scholars study Qur’an memorisation, Qira’ah (Qur’an reading), Deen, Nasheed and Adhkaar. In Primary they add Arabic, Hadith, Seerah and Kitabah, alongside their academic subjects.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Every scholar receives a report sheet each term, and results and report cards are also available on the school’s parent portal.
