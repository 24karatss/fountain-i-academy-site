About Us

Our Story

A school founded on purpose

Fountain-I Academy was founded to give families in Ilorin a school where strong academics and a living Islamic tradition go hand in hand. It was started by parents and educators who wanted one school where children would never have to choose between excellent academics and a sound Islamic upbringing.

Today we teach from Crèche to Basic 5, and we are growing one class at a time, with Secondary opening in the 2027/2028 session.

Our Vision

To raise confident, knowledgeable and God-conscious young people who excel in this life and are prepared for the next.

Our Mission

To deliver the Cambridge curriculum alongside daily Qur'an, Arabic and Islamic Studies, taught by caring, qualified teachers in a safe and disciplined setting.
