FAQ

Islamiyyah questions

How are children grouped?

By age and Qur'an reading level. New scholars take a short, friendly assessment on their first weekend.

Can parents track hifz progress?

Yes. Yes. Teachers keep a hifz record for each child, updated every weekend, and parents receive a short progress report each term.

What should my child bring?

A copy of the Qur'an (mushaf), a notebook and a pen. Boys wear a jalabiyyah or school uniform, and girls wear a hijab and modest dress.

Do classes run during school holidays?

No. Weekend Islamiyyah follows the school calendar and breaks for the same holidays.
