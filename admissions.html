FAQ

Admissions questions

How do I apply?

Pick up an admission form (₦5,000) from the school at No. 26, Tanke Iledu, and return it with the documents in the next answer. You can send us an enquiry first using the form above.

What documents do I need?

Your child’s birth certificate, their last result from their previous school, four passport photographs and a photocopy of their immunisation records.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we encourage it. Book a tour through the form above or call 0810 011 9066.

What are the school hours?

7:30am to 3:30pm, Monday to Friday.

When does each term start?

For 2026/2027: First Term began on 21 September 2026, Second Term begins on 11 January 2027, and Third Term begins on 26 April 2027.

How do I pay school fees?

Pay into GTBank, account name Fountain-I Academy, account number 3005641172, and keep your bank reference. The school issues a receipt for every payment. Books and stationeries are paid once a session.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes. The school runs a bus. Ask us about routes when you enquire.
